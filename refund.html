Return & Refund Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026
Terms & ConditionsReturn & Refund PolicyPrivacy Policy

We want you to love every Levanche piece. This policy explains how returns, exchanges and refunds work, in line with Qatar's Consumer Protection Law No. (8) of 2008 and the Ministry of Commerce and Industry's return and exchange requirements.

Your legal rights come first. If an item is defective, damaged, not as described, or not what you ordered, you are always entitled to a free exchange or a full refund — this right cannot be removed by any store policy.

1. Defective, Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your item arrives damaged or defective (for example torn fabric, broken zip or buttons, faulty stitching), does not match its description, or is not the item, size or colour you ordered:

  • Contact us on or with your order number and a photo of the item, preferably within 7 days of delivery.
  • You may choose a replacement, repair or full refund. If a replacement is not available, we will refund the full price.
  • We cover all delivery and collection costs, and refund any original delivery fee paid.

2. Change of Mind — Exchange or Return

If you simply change your mind or the size doesn't fit, you may request an exchange or return within 7 days of delivery, provided that:

  • The item is unworn, unwashed, unaltered and in its original condition;
  • All original tags and labels are attached, with original packaging where possible;
  • The item is free from perfume, makeup, deodorant or other marks;
  • You provide your invoice or order confirmation.

For change-of-mind returns, the customer is responsible for return delivery costs unless we state otherwise. Exchanges are subject to stock availability; if your preferred size or style is unavailable, we will offer a refund.

3. Items Not Eligible for Change-of-Mind Returns

For hygiene and customisation reasons, the following can only be returned if defective or not as described:

  • Items altered, tailored or made-to-measure at your request (for example custom lengths);
  • Items that have been worn, washed or damaged after delivery;
  • Final-sale or clearance items, where this is clearly stated on the product page before purchase.

4. How to Request a Return or Exchange

  1. Contact us on (WhatsApp) or with your name, order number, the item and the reason.
  2. We will confirm your request and arrange collection or tell you where to send the item.
  3. Once we receive and inspect the item (normally within 2 working days), we will confirm the exchange or refund.

5. Refunds

  • Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Card refunds are processed within 7 working days of approval; the time for the amount to appear depends on your bank.
  • Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer or another method agreed with you.
  • For change-of-mind returns, the original delivery fee is non-refundable. For defective, damaged or incorrect items, the full amount including delivery is refunded.

6. Order Cancellation

You can cancel free of charge before your order is dispatched. Any payment made will be refunded in full to the original payment method.

7. Complaints

If you are not satisfied with how your request was handled, please contact us first so we can put it right. You may also contact the Ministry of Commerce and Industry's consumer protection department through its official channels (moci.gov.qa).
