Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Levanche collects, why we collect it, how we protect it and the rights you have, in line with Qatar's Law No. (13) of 2016 on Personal Data Privacy Protection.

1. Who We Are

The data controller is (Commercial Registration No. ), operator of levanche.qa. For any privacy question or request, contact us at or .

2. Personal Data We Collect

  • Contact & order details: name, phone number, email address, delivery address, items ordered, sizes and order history.
  • Payment details: payment status and transaction references. Card payments are processed by licensed payment providers; we do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Communications: messages you send us by WhatsApp, phone, email or social media, including return and complaint requests.
  • Technical data: basic information such as device type, browser and pages visited, used to keep the website secure and working properly.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data, and our website is not directed at children.

3. Why We Use Your Data

  • To process, deliver and support your orders, returns, exchanges and refunds;
  • To communicate with you about your order and answer your enquiries;
  • To prevent fraud and keep our website and services secure;
  • To meet our legal, accounting and regulatory obligations in Qatar;
  • To send you news and offers — only with your consent. You can opt out at any time.

We process your data with your consent, or where necessary to perform our contract with you or to comply with the law.

4. Sharing Your Data

We never sell your personal data. We share it only where needed to serve you:

  • Delivery and courier partners, to deliver your order;
  • Licensed payment providers, to process payments and refunds;
  • Website hosting and IT service providers, under confidentiality obligations;
  • Government or regulatory authorities in Qatar, where required by law.

Some service providers may store data outside Qatar. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure your data receives adequate protection, as required by Qatari law.

5. Cookies

Our website uses only cookies and similar storage that are essential for it to work. If we introduce analytics or marketing cookies in future, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

6. How We Protect Your Data

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including secure (HTTPS) connections, restricted access and trusted service providers. If a data breach occurs that could seriously harm you, we will notify you and the competent authority as required by law.

7. How Long We Keep Data

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above — for example, order and invoice records for the period required by Qatari commercial and tax rules — and then delete or anonymise it securely.

8. Your Rights

Under Law No. (13) of 2016, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is processed;
  • Access your personal data and receive a copy;
  • Request correction of inaccurate data;
  • Request deletion of your data when it is no longer needed;
  • Withdraw your consent, including to marketing messages, at any time;
  • Object to processing that is not necessary.

To make a request, contact us at . We will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the competent data protection authority in Qatar (National Cyber Security Agency — National Cyber Governance and Assurance Affairs).

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with its effective date.
